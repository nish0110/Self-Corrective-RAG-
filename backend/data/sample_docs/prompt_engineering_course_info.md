# Prompt Engineering and Optimization for Generative AI: Mini Project Info

Course code: UE24AM343AB5. Stream: B.Tech CSE (AI & ML), Semester 5.

Teams have 4 students each. The mini project runs 10 to 12 weeks.

There are 3 reviews:
- Review 1 (10 marks): idea presentation and literature survey, 21 to 28 September.
- Review 2 (15 marks): high level design and data collection, 19 to 25 October.
- Review 3 (15 marks): final demo, 16 to 21 November.

The 40 marks are scaled down to 3 + 3 + 4 = 10 marks in total.

The final submission is a 10 page report in IEEE format.
